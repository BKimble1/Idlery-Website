A physical edition is being prototyped and playtested. It is not finished, has no release date, and is not for sale.

The game

Everything you build, you will have to hold.

Each player raises a village around a Village Center, with a Farmhouse, Market, Barracks and Wall, and every turn asks the same question: spend on what pays you back, or on what keeps you alive?

From the third round the sieges open, and the game stops being about what you have built and starts being about what survives.
