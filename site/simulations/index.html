In development

A growing collection of interactive, browser-based models of real processes. The first is Fab One.

Simulation 01

Fab One: build a chip, layer by layer.

Fab One follows a single silicon wafer through a simplified chip fab until it becomes a working two-transistor inverter.

Every step changes one underlying process model, so the machines, the magnified cross-section, the measurements and the final electrical test all tell the same story. Change the spin speed or the exposure dose, and you change the chip you end up testing.

An illustrative simulation: the equipment is stylised, the layout is conceptual, and process behaviour is simplified from cited sources.

Three ways in

  • Learn

    A guided course through the fab, one step at a time, with a caption for what is happening and why it matters.

  • Explore

    Walk the bay, pick any of its machines, read what it does, and watch it work on a sample wafer.

  • Watch

    A narrated film of the whole journey, with captions and chapters.
