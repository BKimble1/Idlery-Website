About

A studio for things worth making.

Idlery Services LLC is an independent studio making iOS apps, interactive simulations, and games.

What we do

Different kinds of work, one standard.

A ledger for repair shops, a periodic table for people learning chemistry, a siege game with a board, and a simulation of a chip fab. They have little in common except how they are made.

Each project is built to do one thing well and to say plainly what it does. No account where a product does not need one, and no claims a product cannot back up.

Blake Kimble’s own engineering projects live separately, in the portfolio.
