Experiments

Early work, with its limits stated

Interface experiment

Holograph

An iPad launcher that shows apps, websites and folders as tiles behind cyan glass. Optional head-tracked depth and air gestures run on the device; nothing is recorded.

Experimental AR

WallField

Uses the iPhone’s magnetometer and ARKit to map changes in the local magnetic field onto a wall. It does not see through walls, cannot tell whether wiring is present, and its thresholds are still provisional.

Imaging experiment

Turbid

Uses an iPhone’s camera and torch to screen the optical clarity of a water sample. It is a screening tool only: it cannot tell you whether water is safe to drink.
