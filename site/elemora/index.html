In short

More than a wall chart.

A printed table gives you a symbol, a number and a mass. Elemora starts from the same grid and keeps going: what each element is like, what it is used for, and whether you would still recognise it next week.

It is built for students, teachers, and anyone who has decided the table is worth knowing properly. It is a study aid, not a laboratory reference.
