  1. Software · Instrumentation

    Strain-gage lab simulator

    A browser simulator for the ENP 359 strain-gage lab. Place parts on a breadboard, wire a Wheatstone quarter bridge, load a beam, and probe the circuit with a multimeter.

    • BuiltAn offline web app: SVG breadboard, beam view, floating multimeter, and a panel for every lab value.
    • HowEvery reading comes from a DC modified-nodal-analysis solve of the circuit as actually wired. The interface never computes a reading itself.
    • TestedUnit tests with independently derived expectations, and browser tests that drag, wire and probe.
    • Sourcegithub.com/BKimble1/Lab-3-Simulation
