Purchases

Billing and refunds

For App Store purchases and subscriptions, Apple handles the transaction. Manage or cancel a subscription in the Settings app under your Apple Account, and request refunds through Apple. We are still happy to help you work out what happened.

Karnwold’s own payments and refunds are covered by its refund and cancellation policy.
